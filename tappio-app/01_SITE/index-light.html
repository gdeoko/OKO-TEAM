Questions

Frequently asked

Are these apps free to download?
Yes. All three apps are free to download from the App Store. They include premium features unlocked through an optional subscription (weekly, monthly, or yearly).
Do these apps really work?
Yes. Spy Camera Finder uses multiple independent detection methods, Brainova adapts puzzles to your level with a tracked Brain Score, and 3D Tape Measure uses your iPhone's AR for accurate measurements.
Which iPhones are supported?
Any iPhone running a recent version of iOS. 3D Tape Measure offers enhanced precision on LiDAR-equipped models, but all core features work across supported devices.
Is my data collected?
No. All three apps are built privacy-first. Your scans, scores, and measurements stay on your device. We don't sell or share your data.
Can I cancel a subscription anytime?
Yes. Manage or cancel directly in iPhone Settings, Apple ID, Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.
Do I need any technical knowledge?
Not at all. Each app is designed for everyday people. Open it, tap once, and get clear results - with built-in tips when you want to go deeper.
