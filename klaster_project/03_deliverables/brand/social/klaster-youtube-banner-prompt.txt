A wide cinematic 16:9 background plate for a corporate YouTube channel banner of an industrial business park, ultra wide panoramic composition, designed so that a logo and two lines of text can be placed across the exact centre of the frame. The central horizontal band, roughly the middle 50 percent of the width and the middle 45 percent of the height, must stay calm, dark and almost free of detail, with no bright highlights and no busy geometry, so that text placed there stays perfectly legible. All the visual interest lives in the left and right thirds and along the top and bottom edges.

The subject is a modern industrial architecture scene rendered as a dark, expensive, abstracted environment. On the far left third, the suggestion of a long two storey industrial facade receding sharply toward a vanishing point: white perforated metal cassette panels with fine round perforation above continuous ribbon glazing in dark graphite frames, a strict repeating rhythm of panels, heavily softened by atmospheric haze and shallow depth of field so it reads as texture rather than as a photograph. On the far right third, a mirrored counterweight: the dark silhouette of tall production hall gates and a hint of loading dock structure, again abstracted, low contrast, dissolving into shadow toward the frame edge. Between them the scene opens into empty, quiet, dark space.

Colour and light. The base is deep graphite and midnight navy, colour family between #0E1116, #12151C and #1B2130, with a subtle brushed metal grain. A single warm golden light source sits far outside the upper left of the frame and rakes low across the scene, laying a smooth luminous gradient that is brightest in the upper left and falls into rich deep shadow toward the lower right. The gold is a real metallic warm gold in the family #C9A227, #E0BC5A and #8A6A18 in the deepest parts, never yellow plastic, never neon, never orange. Thin razor sharp gold specular lines catch a few panel seams and the top edge of the gates. A faint cool steel blue bounce, around #2C3A52, fills the darkest areas so nothing turns to dead black.

Atmosphere and depth. Fine haze drifts through the light beam. A sparse scattering of small out of focus dust motes catches the gold light in the upper left, rendered as gentle soft bokeh circles, no more than a dozen, tasteful, never a particle storm. The horizon line stays level. A subtle vignette darkens all four corners and naturally frames the empty centre.
