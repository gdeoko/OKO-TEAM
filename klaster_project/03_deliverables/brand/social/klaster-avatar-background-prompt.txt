A premium abstract square background plate for a corporate brand avatar, 1:1 aspect ratio, designed so that a metallic gold emblem can be placed on top of the exact centre. The composition must keep a wide clean circular area of calm negative space in the middle, roughly the central 55 percent of the frame, almost free of detail and free of bright highlights, so that a logo placed there stays perfectly legible at very small sizes. All visual interest lives in the outer ring and the corners.

The subject is a deep, dark, expensive architectural surface. Imagine a wall of dark graphite and midnight navy metal panels, colour between #12151C and #1B2130, with a very subtle brushed metal grain running diagonally, seen at a shallow raking angle so the surface reads as a real physical material rather than flat colour. Across this surface lies a soft, restrained geometric structure: thin precise straight lines and slim rectangular panel seams forming a calm modular grid, like the perforated facade cassettes of a modern industrial building, but heavily abstracted, blurred toward the edges and fading almost completely as they approach the centre. A few of these seams catch a razor thin specular highlight in warm gold.

Lighting is the hero of the image. A single warm golden light source sits far outside the upper left of the frame, raking across the surface at a low angle, producing a smooth luminous gradient that is brightest in the upper left corner and falls off into rich deep shadow in the lower right corner. The gold is a real metallic warm gold, colour family #C9A227, #E0BC5A and #8A6A18 in the deepest parts, never yellow plastic, never neon, never orange. Add a delicate warm bloom in the upper left, a soft anamorphic glow, and a barely visible cool bounce light of steel blue #2C3A52 filling the lower right shadow so the dark side keeps detail and never turns to dead black.

Add depth with atmosphere: a fine haze drifting through the light beam, a scattering of very small out of focus dust motes catching the gold light in the upper left, rendered as gentle soft bokeh circles, sparse and tasteful, no more than a dozen, never a particle storm. Include a slow, elegant sense of volume, as though the plate were lit on a photographic set with a large softbox and a single hard rim light.

Around the outer edge add a subtle circular vignette, darkening the four corners slightly, which naturally frames the empty centre and helps a round profile picture crop cleanly on any platform.
