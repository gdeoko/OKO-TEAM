ПОСТ 1 — АНОНС. Кадр «Ворота открываются».
Формат 4:5, ровно 1080x1350 пикселей, locked, никогда не кропится и не letterbox.
Модель: nano_banana_pro (Higgsfield) либо кабинет ChatGPT, референсом приложить
фотографии площадки из 04_originals и файл логотипа brandbook/logo.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A single cinematic frame shot at the threshold of a working production block in a
south Moscow industrial park at 8:40 in the morning, the moment a four by four
metre roller shutter gate finishes rising and the interior of the hall is opened
to the yard for the first time that day. This is key art for a campaign, not a
documentation photograph: dense, layered, worth a second look, built so that a
person scrolling a feed at thumbnail size stops on it.

CAMERA AND OPTICS. Full frame sensor, 24 mm wide angle, f/4, 1/250 s, ISO 200,
camera 1.1 metres above the poured concrete apron, tilted up four degrees so the
gate opening towers slightly and the verticals of the door frame converge just
enough to read as thrust without looking like a mistake. The camera sits eight
metres back from the gate, outside in the yard, looking straight into the dark
hall through the raised shutter. Anamorphic character: a horizontal flare
stretching off the brightest amber source, oval bokeh in the out of focus
background, a very faint blue fringe on the extreme left and right edges of the
frame. Depth of field runs from the foreground steel lettering at two metres to
the far wall of the hall at forty metres, with the plane of critical focus on the
group of people in the gate opening at eighteen metres.

FOUR DEPTH PLANES, built deliberately.

NEAR FOREGROUND, lower third of the frame, two metres from the lens, slightly
soft. Three dimensional standing letters made of brushed and clear anodised
aluminium, one hundred and eighty millimetres deep with a three millimetre
chamfer on every edge, standing upright on the wet concrete apron with their
faces vertical and square to camera, never lying flat on the ground, the baseline
dead level in frame, every letter exactly the same height. Faces warm white
#F5F1E8 powder coat at roughness 0.35 under a 0.05 clearcoat; the amber #E8A400
lives only on the milled side walls at roughness 0.22 and never touches the
faces. Each letter throws a real hard contact shadow onto the wet apron and each
one is doubled by a soft reflection in the standing water beneath it. The letters
read exactly, character by character, the Russian line «БРОКЕР-ТУР 2 СЕНТЯБРЯ».
This headline block spans at least sixty percent of the frame width and a quarter
of its height, its cap height no less than a twelfth of the frame height, and it
holds at a two hundred pixel thumbnail.

SUBJECT PLANE, eighteen metres in, inside the gate opening. A group of five
people in business outerwear, dark coats and jackets, no hard hats, walking
forward out of the dark hall toward the camera and toward the light. They are
rim lit from behind by the interior of the hall and read as near silhouettes with
warm edges: shoulders, collars, the line of a jaw, a tablet held at hip height in
one hand, a rolled sheet of drawings under another arm. Faces are not resolved
and no one is recognisable; they are shapes in motion, caught mid stride, one
front heel already off the ground. Their contact shadows stretch toward the
camera across the threshold.

MID GROUND, the gate itself and the frame around it. The corrugated steel shutter
is stopped at the top of its travel with the last slat still a hand's width from
the header, the guide rails on both sides scuffed to bright metal by twenty years
of use, the rubber seal at the sill worn and split at one corner. The reveal of
the opening is raw concrete with form tie marks and honest chipping at the
corners, and a black and amber hazard chevron is painted on the sill, half worn
away by tyres. On the pale grey concrete wall to the left of the gate, at head
height, the brand mark is fixed as a real object: a twelve toothed gear wheel
with a clean circular centre holding the stylised figure of a person with arms
raised to both sides, and beside it the wordmark reading exactly «КЛАСТЕР». It is
a milled steel plate with real thickness, its own contact shadow and the same
light as the wall behind it, and it is small — the whole lockup spans under a
seventh of the frame width and its caps are under a third of the headline caps.
It appears once, in that one place, and nowhere else in the picture.

DISSOLVING BACKGROUND, forty metres deep inside the hall. Round painted columns
receding in two rows, a poured seamless floor with old yellow safety marking half
worn away, and the far end of the hall opening into a second bright rectangle of
daylight. Between the columns, four hard shafts of morning light cross the space
diagonally from clerestory windows high on the right wall, and the dust of the
hall turns slowly inside every shaft. Deep in the background, small and out of
focus, a scissor lift and a stack of pallets. Nothing in this plane is sharp.

WHAT IS HAPPENING AT THIS EXACT INSTANT. Something crosses between the planes so
the eye travels: fine construction dust and water vapour roll out of the hall
through the opened gate toward the camera, catching the backlight and forming a
soft luminous wedge across the middle of the frame at knee height. Off to the
left, sixteen metres away and just outside the gate line, a welder is working
behind a temporary screen: a cone of white hot sparks arcs down and left at about
twelve metres per second, the sparks within a metre of the source frozen as hard
white points and those beyond it stretched into short amber traces. On the far
right edge, the rear of a box truck is backing in, its reversing lamps two hot
points and one tail light drawing a short red smear along the wet concrete.
Water still runs off the lower edge of the shutter in three thin threads and
breaks on the sill.

LIGHT. Three sources, stated and distinct. A 5600 K key comes from the open sky
behind the camera and rakes the wet apron so every puddle becomes a mirror. A
6500 K rim source deep inside the hall, from the second doorway at the far end,
cuts the group of people out of the darkness and edges the top of every shoulder.
A 2800 K sodium practical hangs on the wall inside the hall, right of frame,
glowing as a warm point with a soft bloom and throwing a low amber wash across
the floor. Never one flat wash. Amber is allowed to bloom and flare on its own
sources.

MATERIALS AND PHYSICS FLOOR. At least three materials of clearly different
roughness: poured concrete at 0.75, brushed steel at 0.25, wet standing water and
the clearcoat on the letters at 0.02. True reflections and believable contact
shadows everywhere; caustics on the wet concrete under the amber practical.
Black point sits at 6 of 255, so the shadows are deep but never crushed to pure
black. At most two overlaid effects: the dust volume and the spark trails, and
nothing else.

PALETTE. Graphite #14171C in the shadows, the shutter and the coats. Pale warm
grey concrete for the walls and apron, honest and slightly stained. Warm white
#F5F1E8 for the letter faces and the hard daylight. Amber #E8A400 on the letter
side walls, the hazard chevron, the sodium practical and the spark traces. Muted
gold #C9A233 only in the deepest reflections. Rich contrasty grade, film response
rather than digital flatness, fine grain in the shadows.

TYPOGRAPHY DISCIPLINE. Under the main lettering, set small and calm in the same
layout, on the darkened band of wet concrete, a supporting line in warm white
#F5F1E8 condensed capitals at one third of the headline cap height, reading
exactly «11:00 · 6-Я РАДИАЛЬНАЯ, 17 · КОНФЕРЕНЦ-ЗАЛ». Contrast is set, not hoped
for: where any type crosses a bright or busy area the ground beneath it is
darkened. Amber type lives only on graphite or black; on lighter ground the
letters are warm white or near black, never amber. Type is upright always, no
italic, no oblique, no script. The outer guillemets in this prompt are delimiters
and are not drawn. The headline faces camera square, its baseline horizontal,
never rotated, never running up or down the side of the frame. Nothing crosses
the characters, no blur, no unplanned line break, eight percent dead margin all
round. Every Cyrillic letter, digit, space, punctuation mark and the letter Ё
with its two dots is reproduced exactly as written above, without autocorrection
or glyph substitution.

WHAT MUST NOT APPEAR. Nothing drawn from an interface: no buttons, swipe arrows,
link chips, cursors, app icons or screen mock-ups. No text beyond the two lines
given here: no subtitles, extra numbers, scale bars, street names, addresses,
watermarks, timestamps, price tags, currency signs, a second logo, invented
words, Latin placeholder lettering, emoji or icons. No recognisable faces. No
hard hats or high visibility vests on the group: these are brokers, not builders.
No flat backdrop behind a floating object, no illustration or painterly look, no
clip art, no sharpening halo. Do not clad the building in dark perforated panels,
do not turn it into a glass tower, do not add floors: this is an honest working
production block of pale warm grey concrete.

FINISH. Photoreal and physically correct, a high end render married to a
documentary photograph, at the level of a campaign key visual for an industrial
brand. Composition deliberate rather than centred, built on the diagonal that
runs from the lower left lettering through the dust wedge to the group in the
gate. Eight thousand pixels of detail in the plane of focus. The whole frame
should read, at a glance and at thumbnail size, as one sentence: the gate is
open, come in.
