A cinematic key art frame, 16:9, of a single empty production block interior inside a working Moscow industrial park, shot as premium campaign photography for a brokers' event. This is the space a broker walks a client into: clean, high, structural, full of latent value. Photoreal and physically correct, a high end architectural render married to a documentary photograph.

CAMERA. Full frame, 24mm, f/5.6, 1/125s, ISO 320, camera 1.5m high on the poured seamless floor, dead level, one strong one-point perspective straight down the length of the hall so the round painted columns march away in two rows to a bright far wall. Anamorphic character: soft horizontal flare off the brightest window, gentle oval bokeh in the deepest background.

THE SPACE. A production hall on round concrete columns painted industrial grey, a poured seamless floor with old yellow safety markings half worn, a five metre clear height to exposed painted roof trusses and ductwork. Along the right wall, a continuous ribbon of tall industrial windows in dark frames throws four hard diagonal shafts of cold morning light across the floor, and the dust of the hall turns slowly inside them. At the far end, a four by four metre roller shutter gate stands half open onto a bright wet yard. Everything is honest pale warm grey concrete, lightly stained, real, not a glossy showroom.

FOUR DEPTH PLANES. Near foreground, lower left, the cut edge of a machined steel measuring rule laid on the floor catching the raking light, slightly soft. Subject, the columns and the shafts of light down the hall. Mid ground, a lone figure of a person in a dark coat far down the hall, small, standing where the light crosses, giving the enormous scale a human measure, no face resolved. Dissolving background, the half open gate and the pale rectangle of daylight beyond.

WHAT IS HAPPENING. Fine dust drifts through the light shafts. A thin curl of steam rises from a floor drain. Nothing else moves. The frame is calm, vast, waiting.

LIGHT. 5600K cold daylight raking through the right-side windows as the key, a 6500K bounce off the far wall lifting the shadows, a single warm 2800K sodium fixture glowing high on the left wall as a small amber accent. Never one flat wash. Deep blacks, black point at 6 of 255, rich contrasty film grade, fine grain in the shadows.

MATERIALS AND PHYSICS. Poured concrete floor at roughness 0.7 with true soft reflections of the light shafts, painted steel columns at 0.35, glass at 0.02, one machined steel rule at 0.2. Believable contact shadows, real caustics where wet meets the floor near the gate.
